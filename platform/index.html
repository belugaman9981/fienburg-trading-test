What’s a “John Wick” candle, and what else does this look for?

“John Wick” is trader slang for a candle with a very long thin wick and a small body, also called a shooting star or pin bar. A long wick at the top after a rally means buyers pushed the price up and sellers slammed it right back down. A long wick at the bottom after a drop (a hammer) is the reverse. Slowing down shows up as shrinking candles and fading volume while price still rises.
